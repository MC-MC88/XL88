Guide

How to use XL88

Everything you need in one minute.

XL88 is a plain HTML file. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for. Open it, find the formula you need, copy it, and paste it directly into your spreadsheet.

Quick start

  1. Browse the grid or use the search box to find a function by name or purpose.
  2. Click any formula card to open its details.
  3. Fill in the fields with your own cell references or values.
  4. Watch the live preview update as you type.
  5. Click the copy icon and paste the formula into Excel.
